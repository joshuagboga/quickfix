Questions

FAQ

Should I come with my hair washed?

Not necessary — every styling service can include a wash. For braids and installs we recommend booking our wash add-on so your scalp starts fresh.

Do you provide braiding hair and bundles?

Yes. We stock braiding hair, crochet hair and 100% human hair bundles in the shop. You're also welcome to bring your own.

How long does a custom wig take?

Most custom units are ready in 5–10 business days. Hand-ventilated full lace units can take 2–3 weeks.

Can you revive an old wig?

Absolutely. Our wig revamp includes a deep wash, detangle, conditioning treatment, re-plucking, re-bleaching knots and restyling.

Do you work with kids and men?

Yes — we offer kids' braids and styles, plus cuts, line-ups, twists and locs for men.

What is your shipping and return policy?

Orders ship in 1–3 business days. Unworn hair with the lace uncut and in original packaging can be returned within 14 days.
